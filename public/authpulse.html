The tracker

Clock

Due, due today, or late

Each request runs against the timeframe its plan type carries, and turns amber, then red, as the deadline passes.

Flags

Denied without a specific reason

Medicare Advantage, Medicaid and CHIP plans must give a specific reason when they deny a medical service. AuthPulse flags the denials that don't, so staff ask before deciding on an appeal.

Renewals

Approvals expiring soon

Approvals that run out within 30 days are listed before they lapse, so renewals start on time.

Evidence

A record you can take to the plan.

Every request keeps its timeline: submitted, delivery confirmed, calls to the plan, the decision, and any appeal. The turnaround report shows each plan's record over 90 days.

Questions

What AuthPulse does, and doesn't.

A tracker for your staff, and status for your patients.

Does AuthPulse submit prior authorizations?

No. Your team keeps submitting the way it does today: through the EHR, payer portals, or fax. AuthPulse tracks each request from submission to decision and keeps the record. Teams that fax can add the packet-and-fax assistant, a paid upgrade: AuthPulse builds the packet, sends the fax, and the delivery receipt starts the clock. AuthPulse does not submit electronically.

Do we need the DialTone.Med voice agent?

No. AuthPulse works on its own. With the voice agent, verified patients can check status by phone and get a text when a request is approved, denied, needs more information, or an appeal is decided. The voice agent reads that status from AuthPulse, which is 20% off with any DialTone.Med plan.

How do requests get into AuthPulse?

Staff add requests by hand or upload a spreadsheet. If your EHR can export a prior authorization report on a schedule, AuthPulse picks it up on its own, so nothing is retyped. Both are included. Paid upgrades connect your health plans' prior authorization APIs (from 2027) and, where your EHR supports it, a direct EHR integration.

Does AuthPulse decide anything?

No. The plan decides and your team records it. A status in AuthPulse is a fact relayed from the plan, never a prediction.

When is it available, and what does it cost?

AuthPulse is in development, in early access. Practices pay $59 a month per prescriber; specialty pharmacies and billing companies pay $0.50 per request tracked. See the pricing page for minimums and upgrades. Join early access and we'll contact you as customers are onboarded.

Who is it for?

Medical practices and practice groups, specialty pharmacies, and billing companies that work prior authorizations for the practices they serve. Each organization can track several sites in one account.
