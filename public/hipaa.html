Compliance

Security and HIPAA

ByteStreams LLC (d/b/a DialTone.Med) · Last updated · November 13, 2026

Summary

This page summarizes how DialTone.Med protects patient information under its HIPAA compliance policy. Practices can request the full policy and a business associate agreement at hello@bytestreams.ai.

01 Our role

Healthcare practices that use DialTone.Med are covered entities under HIPAA. ByteStreams LLC acts as their business associate: it handles protected health information (PHI) only to provide the service on a practice's behalf.

  • We sign a business associate agreement with a practice before handling its patient information.
  • Vendors that handle PHI on our behalf are required to sign business associate agreements with us. They are listed in the Privacy Policy.
  • Workflows collect and use only the information needed for the request being handled.

02 What happens to patient information

  • On the call. The assistant confirms who is calling, then says only what is safe to say aloud. Balances, medications, and procedures are never read out.
  • By secure link. Appointments, balances, and prior authorization detail open in the patient's browser from a link sent by text. Card payments are handled by the payment processor; card numbers are not read to staff or to the assistant.
  • Decisions. DialTone.Med relays facts. It does not approve refills, decide prior authorizations, or give medical advice.

03 Safeguards

Administrative

  • Annual risk analysis, with the risk management plan updated from its findings
  • Role-based, least-privilege access, reviewed quarterly
  • Incident handling with containment, investigation, remediation, and post-incident review
  • Contingency planning: daily encrypted backups, disaster recovery, and annual testing

Technical

  • AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.2+ encryption in transit, including API and telephony channels
  • Unique user IDs, multi-factor authentication, and automatic logoff
  • Audit logging of access to patient information
  • Integrity controls, including checksums, versioning, and alerts

Physical

  • Cloud data centers with restricted physical access
  • Workstations with automatic lock, full-disk encryption, and secured remote access
  • Documented disposal and sanitization of devices and media

04 Retention

InformationHow long it is kept
Call recordings and transcriptsKept only when the caller agrees to recording, for 30 days, then deleted
Patient and request recordsWhile the practice uses the service; removed or returned when the practice leaves
Audit logs and compliance recordsAt least six years
Card numbersNot stored by DialTone.Med; handled by the payment processor

05 Breach notification

  • Suspected incidents are reported to our compliance officer within 24 hours and assessed using the four HIPAA risk factors in 45 CFR 164.402.
  • As a business associate, we notify affected practices without unreasonable delay and within 30 days.
  • Breach investigations and corrective actions are documented and kept for at least six years.

06 Training and review

  • HIPAA training before access to systems holding PHI, with an annual refresher
  • Policy review at least annually, with quarterly log review and an annual risk assessment
  • Good-faith reporting of concerns is required and protected from retaliation

07 Patient rights

Patients exercise their HIPAA rights through their healthcare practice. We support practices in meeting requests for access, amendment, restriction, confidential communications, and an accounting of disclosures.
