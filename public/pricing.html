Pricing FAQ

How the plan and bands work.

One plan, one rule for volume, and no features held back.

What does the plan include?

Every band includes the full product: a branded voice agent that answers each call, verifies the caller, and texts a secure link for appointments, billing and payment, prior authorization status, and insurance verification. Refill requests are queued for staff. Calls are recorded and tagged by type, and staff work from one console.

What counts as an answered call?

An inbound call with at least one caller reply. Hang-ups and calls without at least one reply are not counted. Text messages sent during a call are included.

What happens if our call volume changes?

Your band is set on a three-month average of answered calls, so one busy month does not move you up. Each band of 750 calls adds $900 a month.

How does the 1.5% payment fee work?

It is 1.5% of the gross amount of each patient payment made through the texted link. Card transaction fees are separate and are paid by the practice. Payments you collect any other way carry no DialTone.Med fee, and the fee is the same in every call band.

Do we need an integration to start?

No. You keep your existing phone numbers, and your staff load balances and prior authorization statuses and manage the calendar in the DialTone.Med console. EMR and EHR connection is scoped to your practice and quoted separately.

How is a multi-location group priced?

Each location has its own plan and band. Groups, and locations above 3,000 answered calls a month, receive a quote.
