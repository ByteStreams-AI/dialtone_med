Data handling

What happens to patient data.

DialTone.Med handles patient information as a business associate, under a signed agreement with your practice. This is what it does with that information, and what it never does.

On the call

Status, not detail

The agent confirms who is calling, then says only what is safe to say aloud. Balances, medications, and procedures are never read out.

By secure link

Detail goes to the patient

Appointments, balances, and prior authorization detail open in the patient's browser. Card payments go straight to the payment processor, so no one reads a card number aloud.

Decisions

Your team decides

DialTone.Med relays facts. It never approves a refill, decides a prior authorization, or gives medical advice.

Retention

Kept briefly, then deleted

Calls are recorded only with the caller's consent. Recordings and transcripts are kept for 30 days. Access records are kept for at least six years.

Encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2+ and at rest, with role-based access, MFA, and audit logging. Controls summarized from the published HIPAA Compliance Policy. Client configuration and contractual requirements apply.
