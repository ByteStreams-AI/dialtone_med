# DialTone.Med for Specialty Pharmacy {.marketing-title}

[Every patient call answered. Every request moved forward.]{.lead}

## Your team already answers nearly every call. That is the cost

Accredited specialty pharmacies abandon only **2.98%** of calls and answer **83%** with a person within 30 seconds. Every one of those answers is staff time, including calls about refills, prior authorization status, and billing.

DialTone.Med answers the call, verifies the caller, and texts a secure link that finishes the request. Your staff see every request, recording, and outcome in one console, and step in only where a person is needed.

## What the call handles, and what it leaves for your team

| Request                    | Said on the call                              | Sent by secure link                                      | Left for your staff                                |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------ | ---------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Prior authorization status | Status only, never the drug or procedure      | What each request is for, its dates, and the next step   | Nothing. No status call to return.                 |
| Refill request             | The request, taken in the patient's own words | Nothing needed                                           | One request to review. The decision stays with your team. |
| Billing and payment        | No dollar amounts                             | The balance and card payment                             | Nothing. The patient gets a receipt by text and email. |
| Insurance update           | An identity check and the offer of a link     | Carrier, member ID, and group number, typed by the patient | The submitted details to review                    |

Appointments are included too: patients book, reschedule, or cancel from the same kind of link.

## What happens to patient data

DialTone.Med handles patient information as a business associate, under a signed business associate agreement with your pharmacy.

- **On the call, status and not detail.** The agent confirms who is calling, then says only what is safe to say aloud. Balances, medications, and procedures are never read out.
- **By secure link, detail goes to the patient.** Balances and prior authorization detail open in the patient's browser. Card payments go straight to the payment processor, so no one reads a card number aloud.
- **Your team decides.** DialTone.Med relays facts. It never approves a refill, decides a prior authorization, or gives medical advice.
- **Kept briefly, then deleted.** Every call opens with a notice that it may be recorded, and a caller can ask to stop recording at any time. Recordings and transcripts are kept for 30 days. Access records are kept for at least six years.

## Your staff see every call

DialTone.Med handles the request. Your team sees what was asked, what was sent, and what still needs a person.

- Calls are tagged by type: refill, prior authorization, billing, insurance, appointment.
- Refill requests, and anything the agent could not finish, arrive as messages in the caller's own words and stay unresolved until staff close them.
- Recordings and transcripts are there unless the caller asked to stop recording.

## Caregivers can call, within limits you set

Many specialty patients have a family member who calls for them. Your staff grant each caregiver only the services they need and approve the phone their links go to. A caregiver who asks for anything else is told it is not available on that call.
